Knob Timer

A Toggl time tracker on a round knob. Tracking mode counts up while you work; Pomodoro mode counts down a set length. Your task and its project sit front and center, and the rim around the dial carries the project's Toggl color. In Pomodoro, the rim is the countdown: it shrinks as time runs out.

Vibration motor: idle 1.8-inch round IPS · 360 × 360 px

    How to use it

    Both modes

    Tap the screen
    Start, pause and resume. Press Space.
    Hold
    Reset: the time goes to 0:00 in Tracking and to the set length in Pomodoro. The task goes back to "Untitled", and the project is cleared to "No project". Press R.
    Tap the task
    Past tasks, newest first, with "Untitled" on top. Turn the knob to scroll, tap to choose. A task too long for the screen scrolls by itself. Press T.
    Tap the project pill
    Your Toggl projects; "Work" is the default. Press P.
    Tap the mic
    Say the task (simulated here). Saying a project's name picks it. Press M.
    Swipe up
    Switch between Tracking and Pomodoro (not while running). Press Shift + ↑.
    Swipe left or right
    Change the color profile: Mono (white on black) or Inverted (black on white). Works any time, even while running. Press Shift + ← →.

    Tracking (counts up)

    The time
    Counts up as MM:SS, then HH:MM:SS from one hour on.
    Pause and resume
    The counter keeps the session total; in Toggl each stretch is its own entry, with the same task and project.
    Turn the knob while running
    Clockwise adds a minute, anticlockwise takes one off, for when you started before (or after) you tapped. Toggl's entry start moves to match. Press → ←.
    Pick a task while running
    Switches: the current entry stops and the new task starts; the counter keeps going.
    Swipe down
    Refresh from Toggl: picks up a timer you started or stopped elsewhere. Press Shift + ↓.

    Pomodoro (counts down)

    Turn the knob
    Sets the length while reset (the rim arc grows and shrinks with it); whole minutes while paused; locked while running.
    The rim
    An arc from 12 o'clock showing the time left out of 60 minutes. It shrinks as time runs out.
    The last ten minutes
    The time fades to Leuchtorange at 5:00 and red at 1:00, and pulses in the last minute. Time's up vibrates at full strength for 30 seconds, or until you touch the screen or turn the knob.

    On the real device

    • Toggl only hears from the knob when you act: start, pause, a new task or project, opening a list, or swiping down. Nothing runs in the background.
    • Entries start in Toggl right away. A stretch shorter than 10 seconds is deleted, so an accidental tap leaves nothing behind.
    • The mic is the only button. The task and the project pill are tappable too.
    • The Wi-Fi glyph sits in the rim at 6 o'clock: steady when connected, pulsing while reconnecting.
    • The projects and tasks here are examples. The knob lists your own, in their Toggl colors.
