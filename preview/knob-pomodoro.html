Knob Pomodoro

A countdown with two hands. The slice between them is the time left, and it shrinks as the moving hand sweeps back to 12. The numbers flip color where the slice crosses them. In the last ten minutes the slice slowly warms up: it reaches Leuchtorange at five minutes and deep red at one. In the last minute the time itself pulses once a second, like a heartbeat. Pick a Toggl project and the rim around the dial takes on its color.

Vibration motor: idle 1.8-inch round IPS · 360 × 360 px

How to use it

Turn the knob
Adds or removes a minute while the timer is paused or reset. When paused, the seconds go back to :00. It's locked while counting down. Drag the ring, scroll over it, or press ← →.
Tap the screen
Starts and pauses. A tap that just misses a button does nothing, so a near miss won't start the timer. Click the screen or press Space.
Hold the screen
Resets to the time you set. Press and hold, or press R.
Tap the mic
While the timer is stopped, tap the mic (bottom) and say what you're working on; a waveform shows it hearing you. It stops listening when you pause, or tap again. The task appears above the time (simulated here). Press M.
Tap the clock
While the timer is stopped, tap the clock badge (top) for your recent Toggl entries, newest first, with "No task" above them to clear the task. Turn the knob to scroll and tap to make one your task. The list is fetched fresh each time. Press T.
Swipe down
Opens your Toggl projects while the timer is stopped. Turn the knob to scroll, tap to choose, swipe up to close. The rim around the dial takes the project's color. Press Shift + ↓ ↑.
Swipe left or right
Changes the slice's color profile while the timer is stopped. Drag across the screen, or press Shift + ← →.

On the real device

  • The full circle is 60 minutes. A 25-minute timer starts as a slice from 12 to 5 o'clock.
  • Time's up is a vibration from the built-in motor. The board has no speaker.
  • Your settings are remembered. The length, color profile and project you pick survive a power-off.
  • Running time goes to Toggl. Each stretch between start and pause becomes a time entry with your task and project. The projects here are examples; the knob lists your own, in their Toggl colors.
  • Recent entries come straight from Toggl. The clock button fetches your latest finished entries (up to 50) every time you press it.
  • The Wi-Fi icon is steady when connected and pulses while reconnecting. Try the placements under Preview options.
